Stage Expression Profiles

Time or Stage: Development post P0 division based on the times given by Sulston et al. dcpm: Depth of coverage per million reads (see Suppl. Methods in Gerstein et al, 2010).

Time or Stage

Expression (dcpm)

In the expression field to specify a range use '>0.5' or '<0.5' or '2-10'. To compare relative expression levels between stages specify x-fold difference and stage for comparison in the following way: '>10x adult' translates into 'more than 10-fold higher than in the 'adult' stage' (compared to the stage specified by the value in the pull-down menu. Use values from the pull-down menu (e.g. '44 min', or 'Dauer entry') to specify times or stages.

Tissue Expression Profiles

Expression values are given in transcripts per million (TPM)

Tissue or Cell Type

expression (TPM)

To specify a range use '>0.5' or '<0.5' or '2-10'. To compare relative expression levels between tissues specify x-fold difference and tissue for comparison in the following way: '>10x Body wall muscle' means expression in the tissue specified by the value in the pull-down menu is 'more than 10-fold higher than expression in body wall muscle'. Use exact values from the pull-down menu (e.g. 'Body wall muscle') to specify the tissue for comparison.
