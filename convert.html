Convert

Convert C. elegans gene and protein identifiers.

Input Identifiers

The IDs will be converted into the following:

  • Wormbase gene ID (WBGene00003738)
  • Gene Sequence Name (F54F3.1)
  • Locus (nid-1)
  • Protein Sequence Name (F54F3.1a)
  • Wormpep ID (CE18731)
  • Uniprot ID (Q93791)
