To specify a range use '>0.5' or '<0.5' or '2-10'. To compare relative expression levels between tissues specify x-fold difference and tissue for comparison in the following way: >10x neuron_t3' means expression in the tissue/time point specified by the values in the pull-down menus is 'more than 10-fold higher than expression in neurons at t3'. Use values from the pull-down menus and seperate tissue and timepoint by an underscore (e.g. 'neuron_t3').
